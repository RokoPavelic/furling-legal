Common questions

My pet is taking a long time to build

Filming the animations takes a few minutes, and it keeps going if you close the app. If it has been much longer than that, force-quit and reopen — it picks up where it left off rather than starting over, and it will not charge you twice.

I paid but nothing unlocked

Open the paywall again and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases belong to your Apple ID, so they come back even after reinstalling. If that does not work, email us.

I reinstalled and my pet is gone

If you used Keep your pet forever before reinstalling, open it again and sign in with the same email address — your pet comes back.

If you never backed up, your pet lived only on that phone and deleting the app deleted it. Anything you paid for is not lost — use Restore Purchases and you can create again.

How do I delete my backup?

Email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account, your backup and everything stored with it within 30 days.

How do I cancel Furling+?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Furling. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete everything?

In Furling, open Settings and tap Erase everything on this device. That removes your pet, its videos, your friends and your album. It cannot be undone.

Where do my photos go?

To the AI services that draw and animate your pet, and nowhere else. The Privacy Policy names each one.
